# Product Roadmap

## Sections

### 1. Pipeline Dashboard
The home surface for BD reps — stat cards (prospects, spend analyzed, opportunities, engagement value), the prospect/project list, a kanban of opportunities by stage (identified → proposal generated → sent → viewed → meeting scheduled), and a renewal calendar with 90/60/30-day urgency bands.

### 2. Prospects & SaaS Portfolio
Per-project workspace for capturing the engagement: branded intake form or rep-guided entry, the project's SaaS list with manual entry plus bulk CSV import, structured contract/financial form per app with PDF upload + AI extraction, and SaaS Catalog autocomplete pre-populated with feature checklists.

### 3. Surveys & Interviews
Magic-link survey delivery and response review across the three survey types (Tool Intake, Financial, Usage), public blast links shareable in Slack, server-side progress persistence for cross-device resume, and reviewer surfaces for standout-respondent quotes and follow-up transcript uploads.

### 4. Opportunities & Build Scoping
Per-SaaS Rip Opportunity detail showing the 0–100 score with breakdown of the six weighted factors, redundancy callouts and category taxonomy, and the AI-generated build scope (narrative, feature list with complexity, integration plan, tech stack, team/duration, cost range, timeline).

### 5. Proposals
Consultant-grade proposal builder and viewer: executive summary, current-state analysis, "Ravn alternative" section, year-by-year financial comparison, risk mitigation, and engagement options. Web share view, PDF export, Google Slides export, and view tracking that fires owner notifications on each open.
