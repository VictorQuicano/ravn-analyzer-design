# Analyzer

## Description
Analyzer is Ravn's lead-generation and sales-discovery workspace. It helps BD reps and Partners identify enterprises overpaying for underutilized SaaS, quantify the waste through structured power-user interviews, and generate consultant-grade proposals that convert vendor renewals into custom-build engagements.

## Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Enterprise SaaS waste is invisible
Companies spend ~$4,830 per employee per year on SaaS with roughly half of seats unused, but the data never reaches the people deciding renewals. Analyzer captures portfolio, contract, and per-seat utilization in one place so the waste becomes a number on a page.

### Problem 2: Decision-makers haven't caught up to AI-era build economics
AI-assisted development has compressed custom-build timelines 5–10x, flipping ROI math that used to favor "buy" toward "build". Analyzer turns each opportunity into a side-by-side 5-year TCO comparison so Ravn's argument is grounded in numbers, not slogans.

### Problem 3: Lead generation isn't repeatable
Ravn doesn't have a systematic pipeline for finding companies with real budget and pain. Analyzer turns prospect intake → surveys → scoring → proposals into a single repeatable workflow with a kanban-style pipeline view.

### Problem 4: Sales conversations lack ammunition
Partners walk into renewal conversations with vague pitch decks. Analyzer arms them with utilization charts, verbatim user-pain quotes, redundancy callouts, and polished web/PDF/Slides proposals that read like management consulting deliverables.

### Problem 5: Renewal timing is the forcing function nobody tracks
Contract renewals are the natural moment to challenge a vendor, but they slip past. Analyzer surfaces upcoming renewals on a calendar with 90/60/30-day urgency bands and triggers Slack alerts so reach-out timing is intentional.

## Key Features
- Branded prospect intake (self-service form + rep-guided entry)
- SaaS portfolio capture with bulk CSV and master-catalog autocomplete
- Magic-link interview surveys (Tool Intake / Financial / Usage) with public blast links and cross-device progress save
- 0–100 Rip Opportunity Score with six weighted factors and Hot/Warm/Lukewarm/Cold tiering
- AI-generated build scoping (feature list, complexity, integrations, tech stack, team, cost range, timeline)
- AI-generated proposals with web view, PDF, and Google Slides export plus per-recipient view tracking
- Pipeline dashboard with stat cards, kanban, and renewal calendar
- Outbound trigger system (interview reminders, hot-lead Slack alerts, renewal alerts, stale-opportunity nudges)
- Contract PDF upload with AI extraction to auto-fill financial form
- Redundancy detection and category taxonomy across a project's SaaS portfolio
- Project-scoped workspace with tabbed inner surfaces (portfolio / surveys / opportunities / proposals)
